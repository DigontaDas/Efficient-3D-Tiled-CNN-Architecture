## Replacement Section 1: Methods (Dataset, Architecture, Training Protocols, Checkpoints & Evaluation Regimes)

### 1.1 Dataset, Disjoint Split Partitioning, and Cohort Exclusions
All V1 model training and benchmark evaluations were conducted on the public ImageCAS 3D coronary computed tomography angiography (CCTA) dataset (`1,000` volumetric scans; native `LAS` orientation, in-plane spacing `0.29–0.45 mm`, slice thickness `0.50 mm`). Using the canonical split manifest (`Phase3_Local_Integration/splits_final.json`, `SHA-256: efe3f06cfe...`), `934` scans were partitioned into three mutually disjoint subsets:
- **Training Split (`N = 690`)**: Subset of cases `1–850` used for 200-epoch network optimization.
- **Validation Split (`N = 94`)**: Disjoint subset of cases `1–850` used for model development, best-checkpoint selection, validation-only threshold tuning (`τ*`), and the controlled final epoch-200 endpoint comparison.
- **Retrospective Test Split (`N = 150`)**: Contiguous cases `851–1000`. Because earlier V1 developmental sweeps (`τ = 0.60`, loss calibration `α = 0.4, γ = 2.5`) and Phase H explorations inspected cases `851–1000`, all V1 evaluations on Test-150 are reported strictly as **retrospective reassessments**, not untouched confirmatory tests.

Sixty-six ImageCAS cases (`IDs` within `1..200` omitted from `splits_final.json`, previously labeled `unseen_66`) and the historical local hospital QCA cohort (`N = 21` verified / `N = 32` pre-quarantine) are **excluded from all supporting V1 claims** due to legacy training-set overlap (`850`-case nnU-Net `dataset.json`) and unresolved clinical/DICOM pipeline audit issues (`AUDIT_FINDINGS.md`: `S1–S6`, `E5`, `D2`, `D4`, `I2`, `I5`).

### 1.2 RASNet Architecture, `StenosisAwareLoss`, and Verified Parameter Counts
`RASNet` (`Phase3_Local_Integration/rasnet_model.py`, `4,706,772` parameters = `4.707M`) builds on MONAI's 3D `SegResNet` (`init_filters = 16`, encoder blocks `[1, 2, 2, 4]`, decoder blocks `[1, 1, 1]`, `GroupNorm`, `dropout_prob = 0.1`) by adding:
1. **3D Additive Skip-Connection Attention Gates (`AttentionGate3D`)** at the three decoder levels (`64`, `32`, `16` channels), adding `5,858` parameters (`+0.12%`) over the `4,700,914`-parameter (`4.701M`) `SegResNet` baseline.
2. **Multi-Scale Deep Supervision** with auxiliary `1×1×1` heads at Decoder Level 2 (`aux2`, `1/4` resolution) and Level 1 (`aux3`, `1/2` resolution) supervised via `total_loss = loss_main + 0.4 * aux2_loss + 0.2 * aux3_loss` (`train_rasnet_200ep.py:215`).
3. **`StenosisAwareLoss`** (`Phase3_Local_Integration/rasnet_loss.py`): `0.4 * DiceLoss(softmax=True, to_onehot_y=True, include_background=True)` + `0.6 * FocalLoss(gamma=2.5, to_onehot_y=True, include_background=True)`.

Across all five V1 architectures, exact parameter counts verified by instantiating each network and strictly loading its checkpoint weights are: **`SegResNet` (`4,700,914`, `4.701M`) < `RASNet` (`4,706,772`, `4.707M`) < `3D U-Net` (`4,805,534`, `4.806M`) < `nnU-Net V2` (`30,785,994` unique parameters, `30.786M`, `6.54×` larger than RASNet) < `V-Net` (`45,599,452`, `45.599M`)**. Thus, RASNet is `6.54×` (`84.7%`) smaller in parameter count than this `3d_fullres` nnU-Net configuration, while remaining `5,858` parameters larger than its `SegResNet` backbone.

### 1.3 Matched 200-Epoch Training Protocols and Checkpoint Provenance
All five models were trained for `200` epochs on the identical `N = 690` training split under architecture-specific protocols verified directly from training scripts and logs:
- **`RASNet`** (`train_rasnet_200ep.py`): `0.5 mm` isotropic foreground-cropped volumes (`HU [-100, 800] -> [0, 1]`), `96³` patches (`num_samples = 2`), `StenosisAwareLoss(alpha=0.4, gamma=2.5)` + deep supervision, `AdamW(lr = 2e-4, wd = 1e-5)`, `CosineAnnealingLR(T_max = 200)`. Validation ran full-volume `96³` sliding-window inference (`overlap = 0.25`) across all `94` Val cases at `12` checkpoints (`epochs 20, 40, 50, 60, 80, 100, 120, 140, 150, 160, 180, 200`), selecting `rasnet_best.pth` (`114` keys) at epoch `160` (`val_dice = 0.7770`). The recovered final checkpoint `rasnet_last.pth` (`114` keys, `SHA-256: 71c9e5ef...`) contains raw state-dict weights without embedded epoch metadata (`embedded_epoch_metadata_present = False`); its epoch-200 identity is corroborated by unconditional epoch-end saving (`train_rasnet_200ep.py:268`), the contiguous 200-epoch `rasnet_200ep_log.csv` (`ep 200 val_dice = 0.7761`), `CosineAnnealingLR` trajectory alignment, and split-archive timestamp delta (`5120.00 s` vs `5119.97 s` logged elapsed time).
- **`SegResNet` and `V-Net`** (`train_segresnet_200ep.py`, `train_vnet_200ep.py`): Identical `0.5 mm` grid and `96³` patches (`num_samples = 2` for SegResNet; `num_samples = 1` + gradient clipping `max_norm = 1.0` for V-Net), trained with **`DiceFocalLoss(to_onehot_y=True, softmax=True, include_background=False, lambda_dice=1.0, lambda_focal=2.0)`** and **`AdamW(lr = 1e-4, wd = 1e-5)`** with `CosineAnnealingLR`. Full-volume `Val94` validation ran at `20` checkpoints (`every 10 epochs`, best ep `190` = `0.7521`, `83` keys) for SegResNet and `13` checkpoints (`[10, 20, 40, 50, ..., 200]`, best ep `180` = `0.7491`, `164` keys) for V-Net.
- **`3D U-Net`** (`train_3dunet_200ep.py`): Full volumes resized to `128³` (`HU [-100, 800] -> [0, 1]`), `DiceCELoss(sigmoid=True)`, `AdamW(lr = 1e-4, wd = 1e-5)`, `21` validation checks (`[1, 10, 20, ..., 200]`) evaluated on the `128³` resized grid (`3dunet_best.pth`, `63` keys, `epoch = 180`, `best_val_dice = 0.6638`, no `optimizer_state_dict`).
- **`nnU-Net V2`** (`prepare_and_run_nnunet_200ep.py`, `nnUNetTrainer_200epochs`, fold `0`): `3d_fullres` `PlainConvUNet` (`292` `network_weights` keys across `88` shared-storage aliases, `30,785,994` unique parameters), verified `plans.json` patch size **`[96, 160, 160]`**, target spacing **`[0.5, 0.349609375, 0.349609375] mm`**, `CTNormalization`, `DC_and_CE_loss` + deep supervision, `SGD(lr = 1e-2, momentum = 0.99, nesterov = True)` with PolyLR. Validation ran every epoch (`200` evaluations) over `50` batches (`batch_size = 2`, `33.3%` forced foreground oversampling) using batch-pooled `2*TP / (2*TP + FP + FN)` (`Pseudo dice` / `mean_fg_dice`) smoothed by `MetaLogger` (`ema[t] = 0.9*ema[t-1] + 0.1*mean_fg_dice[t]`). Raw `Pseudo dice` peaked at epoch `189` (`0.8376`), whereas `checkpoint_best.pth` (`nnunet_best.pt`) is selected strictly on `ema_fg_dice` and records **`current_epoch = 199`** (`_best_ema = 0.816239`); `checkpoint_final.pth` (`nnunet_last.pt`) records **`current_epoch = 200`** (`mean_fg_dice = 0.8145`, `ema_fg_dice = 0.8161`).

### 1.4 Physical-Grid Evaluation (`surface-v1`) and Strictly Separated Regimes
All native-grid evaluations invert predicted probabilities onto each scan's original SimpleITK `(Z, Y, X)` physical grid and compute true 6-connected boundary distances (`HD95`, `ASD` via `surface-v1` in `benchmark/metrics.py`) and centerline overlap (`clDice`, `centerline_recall`). Because V1 spans distinct checkpoint roles, cohorts, sliding-window protocols, and post-processing settings, results are kept strictly separated across four regimes:
- **Regime A (Final Epoch-200 Val94 Endpoint, `N = 94`, `2` models):** `final_epoch200` checkpoints (`rasnet_last.pth`, `nnunet_last.pt`), symmetric 1-pass, `0.50` Gaussian sliding-window overlap, `τ = 0.50`, no post-processing.
- **Regime B (Best-Checkpoint Val94 References, `N = 94`):** `B1` (`val_full_94` Arm A, `5` models, asymmetric SW `0.25` constant vs `0.50` Gaussian, `τ = 0.50`, no CC) and `B2` (Option A Val94 grid, `4` sliding-window models excluding `3D U-Net`, symmetric `0.50` Gaussian SW, `τ = 0.50`, 26-connected Top-2 CC).
- **Regime C (Retrospective Test150 3-Arm Benchmark, `N = 150`, `IDs 851–1000`, `5` models, `best` checkpoints):** Arm A (asymmetric SW, 1-pass, `τ = 0.50`, no CC), Arm B (asymmetric SW + asymmetric native TTA: RASNet 4-pass, nnU-Net 8-pass, others 1-pass), Arm C (asymmetric SW, 1-pass, Val94-locked `τ*` clamped at `0.30` lower bound + Top-2 CC).
- **Regime D (Option A Symmetric Test150, `N = 150`, `IDs 851–1000` retrospective, `4` sliding-window models, `3D U-Net` excluded):** Symmetric `0.50` Gaussian SW across secondary recipes `R1` (1-pass, `τ = 0.50`, no CC), `R2` (8-pass logit-averaged TTA, `τ = 0.50`, no CC), `R3` (1-pass, Val94-locked `τ*` in `[0.20, 0.70]` + Top-2 CC), and **preregistered primary `R4_tuned_tta8_top2`** (8-pass logit-averaged TTA, Val94-locked `τ*`, Top-2 CC), with pre-specified Dice non-inferiority margin `−0.005` (`RASNet − nnU-Net`) and Holm-adjusted hypothesis families (`FA` primary, `FB/FC/FD` secondary).

### 2.1 Training Telemetry Reconciliation (`fig3_corrected_training_and_val_convergence_200ep`)
Figure 3 (corrected) separates the non-comparable training losses (Panel A, unshifted dual y-axes: positive `StenosisAwareLoss` / `DiceFocalLoss(1.0, 2.0)` / `DiceCELoss` vs. negative nnU-Net `DC_and_CE_loss`) from the logged validation trajectories (Panel B). ### 2.2 Regime A: Final Epoch-200 Val94 Endpoint Comparison (`N = 94`)
Evaluating both `final_epoch200` checkpoints (`rasnet_last.pth` via script/log/archive provenance; `nnunet_last.pt` with embedded `current_epoch = 200`) under the identical single-pass `0.50` Gaussian sliding-window protocol (`τ = 0.50`, no CC, `94/94` valid cases, `0` empty predictions, `188/188` verified mask headers) yielded:
- **Dice (descriptive primary):** `RASNet` **`0.7792 ± 0.0569`** (median `0.7921`) vs. `nnU-Net V2` **`0.7767 ± 0.0562`** (median `0.7824`), paired difference **`+0.0025`** (`95%` bootstrap CI `[-0.0027, +0.0075]`, `54W / 0T / 40L`, exploratory unadjusted Wilcoxon `p = 0.2401`). This difference is not statistically significant and **does not establish Dice superiority or equivalence**.
- **Precision vs. Recall Trade-Off:** All 8 metric p-values in the endpoint table are **exploratory and unadjusted**. Under this unpruned `τ = 0.50` operating point, `RASNet` achieved higher **Precision** (`0.7586` vs. `0.7428`, diff `+0.0158`, `95%` CI `[+0.0072, +0.0245]`, `66W / 0T / 28L`, unadjusted `p = 1.07e-4`), whereas `nnU-Net V2` achieved higher voxel **Recall** (`0.8203` vs. `0.8065`, diff `-0.0138`, `95%` CI `[-0.0175, -0.0098]`, `16W / 0T / 78L`, unadjusted `p = 4.85e-10`) and higher **Centerline Recall** (`0.9142` vs. `0.9068`, diff `-0.0074`, `95%` CI `[-0.0124, -0.0025]`, `37W / 2T / 55L`, unadjusted `p = 0.0092`).
- **Boundary, Topology, and Post-Hoc Multiplicity Check:** `HD95` (`17.32` vs. `18.67 mm`, diff `-1.35 mm`, `95%` CI `[-3.83, +0.95]`, unadjusted `p = 0.6742`), `ASD` (`2.619` vs. `2.748 mm`, diff `-0.128 mm`, `95%` CI `[-0.362, +0.110]`, unadjusted `p = 0.3372`), and `clDice` (`0.8413` vs. `0.8347`, diff `+0.0067`, `95%` CI `[-0.0006, +0.0135]`, unadjusted `p = 0.0352`) did not establish topological or surface superiority. Note that the percentile bootstrap `95%` CI targets the **mean paired difference** whereas the Wilcoxon signed-rank test targets **paired rank location**; these target different quantities and need not coincide (as seen for `clDice`). In a separate **post-hoc exploratory (not preregistered)** Holm-Bonferroni adjustment across all 8 endpoint metrics (`table_epoch200_val94_posthoc_holm8_exploratory.md`), only `Precision` (`Holm-8 p = 7.47e-4`) and `Recall` (`Holm-8 p = 3.88e-9`) remain `< 0.05`, while `clDice` (`Holm-8 p = 0.1760`) and `Centerline Recall` (`Holm-8 p = 0.0551`) do not.

### 2.3 Regime B & Regime C: Best-Checkpoint Val94 and Retrospective Test150 (3-Arm Benchmark)
Under best-checkpoint evaluation (`rasnet_best` ep `160`, `nnunet_best` `current_epoch = 199` under `_best_ema = 0.8162`):
- On `Val94`, asymmetric `Arm A` (`B1`) gave Dice `0.7789 ± 0.0521` (`RASNet`) vs. `0.7761 ± 0.0567` (`nnU-Net`), whereas Option A's symmetric `0.50` Gaussian + Top-2 CC grid at `τ = 0.50` (`B2`) gave `0.7953 ± 0.0657` (`RASNet`) vs. `0.8070 ± 0.0585` (`nnU-Net`).
- On retrospective `Test150` (`Regime C`), raw unpruned `Arm A` (`0.7736` vs. `0.7687`) and native-TTA `Arm B` (`0.7807` vs. `0.7760`) showed higher Precision (`+0.0642` / `+0.0602`) and lower HD95/ASD for `RASNet` over `nnU-Net`, whereas `Arm C` (Val94-locked `τ*` + Top-2 CC) favored `nnU-Net V2` on Dice (`0.8055 ± 0.0633` vs. `0.7899 ± 0.0677`), `HD95` (`8.26` vs. `9.94 mm`, Holm `p = 4.2e-4`), and `ASD` (`1.395` vs. `1.671 mm`, Holm `p = 1.5e-4`), with statistically indistinguishable Precision (`0.8252` vs. `0.8207`, Holm `p = 0.160`). Because `Arm A–C` retained inference asymmetries (`0.25` constant vs. `0.50` Gaussian SW; `4-pass` vs. `8-pass` TTA; `τ = 0.30` grid floor), they are supplemented by Option A (`Regime D`).

### 2.4 Regime D: Option A Symmetric 4-Model Evaluation on Retrospective Test150 (`N = 150`)
When all four sliding-window best checkpoints (`3D U-Net` excluded) were evaluated under identical `0.50` Gaussian sliding-window inference and logit-averaged 8-pass mirroring (`R4_tuned_tta8_top2`, Val94-locked `τ* = 0.40` for RASNet, `0.20` for nnU-Net, `0.45` for SegResNet/V-Net, plus Top-2 CC):
- **Primary Non-Inferiority Verdict (`R4`):** `nnU-Net V2` achieved `0.8059 ± 0.0630` Dice vs. `RASNet` `0.8039 ± 0.0691` (mean diff `-0.0021`, `95%` CI `[-0.0088, +0.0030]`, `76W / 0T / 74L`, Holm `p = 0.990`). Because the lower CI bound (`-0.0088`) crosses the pre-specified `-0.005` margin, **Dice non-inferiority was NOT established (`non_inferior = False`, `superior = False`)**.
- **Primary & Secondary Hypothesis Families (`R4`):** In primary family `FA`, `RASNet` achieved significantly higher **Precision** (`0.8302` vs. `0.8084`, diff `+0.0218`, `95%` CI `[+0.0143, +0.0278]`, `132W / 0T / 18L`, Holm `p = 7.23e-17`), while `HD95` (`8.93` vs. `8.17 mm`, diff `+0.757 mm`, Holm `p = 0.489`) and `ASD` (`1.570` vs. `1.390 mm`, diff `+0.180 mm`, Holm `p = 0.489`) did not differ significantly. In secondary `FD`, `nnU-Net V2` retained higher **Recall** (`0.8104` vs. `0.7861`, diff `-0.0244`, Holm `p = 2.40e-17`, `17W / 0T / 133L`), while `clDice` (`0.8750` vs. `0.8744`, Holm `p = 0.446`) was non-significant. Against matched `SegResNet` (`FB`: `0.7989` Dice) and `V-Net` (`FC`: `0.7915` Dice), `RASNet` achieved higher Dice (`+0.0050`, Holm `p = 4.27e-8`; `+0.0124`, Holm `p = 3.58e-12`) and Precision (`+0.0102`, Holm `p = 3.17e-6`; `+0.0085`, Holm `p = 7.43e-3`).
- **Secondary Option A Recipes (`R1–R3`):** In unpruned secondary recipes `R1` (`0.7788` vs. `0.7687`, diff `+0.0101`) and `R2` (`0.7863` vs. `0.7760`, diff `+0.0103`), `RASNet` exceeded `nnU-Net` on Dice, whereas `R3` (1-pass + `τ*` + Top-2 CC: `0.8008` vs. `0.8055`, diff `-0.0047`) did not establish non-inferiority (`NI = False`). All `R1–R3` comparisons remain strictly secondary to `R4`.

## Replacement Section 3: Computational Efficiency and Runtime Scope
On an `NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4080 SUPER` (`16,376 MiB` VRAM), efficiency is reported separately by measurement scope (`table_v1_efficiency_and_timing_verified.md`):
- **Model Size & Single-Patch Compute (`Table 4`):** `RASNet` (`4.707M` params, `18.00 MiB` checkpoint) requires `123.39 GFLOPs` per `96³` patch, `15.68 ± 0.32 ms` (FP32) / `13.71 ± 0.12 ms` (AMP) patch latency, and `390.7 MB` peak patch VRAM. `nnU-Net V2` (`30.786M` params, `235.05 MiB` checkpoint, `6.54×` larger) requires `1,119.34 GFLOPs` per native `96×160×160` patch (`9.07×` higher) or `402.96 GFLOPs` on an equal `96³` input (`3.27×` higher), `59.56 ± 0.41 ms` (FP32) / `31.52 ± 0.30 ms` (AMP), and `1,926.5 MB` peak patch VRAM.
- **End-to-End Full-Volume Runtime (`Option A timing_summary.csv`):** Measured over `n = 10` Val cases (after `1` warm-up) under `0.50` Gaussian SW (`sw_batch_size = 8` for MONAI vs `1` for nnU-Net) including preprocessing and native-grid inversion, `RASNet` runs in median **`5.40s`** `[5.16, 5.72]` (1-pass, peak VRAM `4,782 MB`) and **`22.95s`** `[22.66, 23.06]` (TTA-8, `5,134 MB`), compared with `nnU-Net V2` at **`29.84s`** `[28.25, 31.69]` (1-pass, `2,243 MB`) and **`74.37s`** `[73.06, 76.28]` (TTA-8, `2,261 MB`). Notably, Option A full-volume peak VRAM is higher for MONAI models (`4.78–5.93 GB`) than for nnU-Net (`2.24–2.26 GB`) due to `sw_batch_size = 8` and GPU tensor accumulation. Resumed-run `manifest.json` (`rasnet = 0.0s`) and legacy hardcoded estimates (`~1.85s`) are excluded as invalid latency numbers.

## Replacement Section 4: Limitations & Conclusions

1. **Retrospective Test Cohort (`Test150`):** Cases `851–1000` were previously inspected during V1 threshold and loss development; V1 `Test150` results are retrospective reassessments rather than untouched confirmatory tests.
2. **Primary Non-Inferiority Not Met Under Symmetric `R4`:** Under Option A's pre-specified primary comparison (`R4`), RASNet did not meet the `-0.005` Dice non-inferiority margin against `nnU-Net V2` (`-0.0021`, `95%` CI `[-0.0088, +0.0030]`), and nnU-Net retained significantly higher recall (`+0.0244`).
3. **Epoch-200 Endpoint Scope & Missing Intermediate Checkpoints:** The Val94 epoch-200 evaluation characterizes final epoch `200` on a development cohort only (`p = 0.2401` establishes neither superiority nor equivalence); `rasnet_last.pth` relies on script/log/archive provenance rather than embedded epoch metadata; and no intermediate checkpoints (`epochs 50, 100, 150`) exist to reconstruct full-volume trajectories across training.
4. **No Causal Architectural Generalization or Clinical/External Proof:** V1 training protocols differed in patch size, spacing, normalization, optimizer, and LR; thus, V1 cannot isolate attention gates or parameter capacity as causal drivers of generalization across regimes. Likewise, because `unseen_66` and the hospital QCA cohort (`N = 21/32`) are excluded due to leakage and unresolved pipeline audit issues, V1 provides **no** independent multi-center external validation, clinical stenosis-grading validation, or HeartFlow/`FFR_CT` comparison.
5. **Conclusion:** Within V1 ImageCAS evaluation, `RASNet` demonstrates a compact `4.707M`-parameter footprint, lower single-patch GFLOPs and faster end-to-end inference than `nnU-Net V2` (`30.786M`), competitive full-volume Dice, and consistent Precision advantages at specified operating points (`τ = 0.50` unpruned and Option A `R4`), while `nnU-Net V2` retains Recall advantages. Confirmatory multi-seed training under controlled symmetric protocols (`Rasnet-v2`) and prospective independent multi-center validation remain required future work.
