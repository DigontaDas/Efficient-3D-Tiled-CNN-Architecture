## Existing evaluation paths and differences

The primary `evaluate_matched_200ep_models.py:197-202,266-271,335-340,403-408` applies RAS orientation absent in the four training validations, and the three patch models omit `CropForegroundd`; primary overlap is 0.5 (`:228,366,436`) instead of 0.25. 3D U-Net resizes after orientation and does not apply training's label transform (`:266-299`). RASNet additionally uses flip TTA, threshold 0.6 and top-two components (`:436-455`); baseline outputs are not equivalent. nnU-Net uses its own predictor and best checkpoint (`:469-486`).

`THESIS_RECOVERY/scripts/02_fair_benchmark_evaluator.py:159-161,212-214,251-256,284-296` similarly canonicalizes RAS and omits training crop; 3D U-Net resizes, uses 0.5 overlap and varies mirroring by arm. `Q1_Publication_Package/ablation/evaluate_ablation_suite.py:67-69,100-107` adds RAS orientation, omits crop and uses 0.5 overlap plus optional TTA. External 3D-CAS paths `3d Cas Validations and doings/scripts/04_evaluate_models_3d_cas.py:186-197,240-241` and `evaluate_unseen_66_models.py:173-183,207-229,272-291` vary orientation, spacing, resize, threshold, overlap and dust pruning; these are not evidence of ImageCAS benchmark parity. Other archived evaluation scripts are historical and have not been exhaustively validated; use their actual transform and checkpoint provenance before relying on them. This inventory is not a claim that every historical script has been individually verified.

### Resolved Lab Conflicts & Provenance Verification
1. **Training-Validation vs. Historical Evaluator (`evaluate_matched_200ep_models.py`) Conflict Resolution**:
   - **Transform chain**: `train_rasnet_200ep.py`, `train_segresnet_200ep.py`, and `train_vnet_200ep.py` trained and validated with `LoadImaged -> EnsureChannelFirstd -> CropForegroundd(source_key="image") -> Spacingd(0.5, 0.5, 0.5, mode="bilinear") -> ScaleIntensityRanged(-100, 800, 0, 1, clip=True) -> EnsureTyped` and `sliding_window_inference(..., roi_size=(96, 96, 96), overlap=0.25)` with **no** `Orientationd(axcodes="RAS")`. By contrast, `evaluate_matched_200ep_models.py` inserted `Orientationd(axcodes="RAS")`, omitted `CropForegroundd`, and used `overlap=0.5`. All 1,000 ImageCAS volumes are natively `LAS`; our benchmark adapter (`benchmark/adapters/monai_models.py`) strictly follows the training-validation recipe (`CropForegroundd`, no `Orientationd`, `overlap=0.25`) and inverts continuous foreground probabilities back onto the native `(Z, Y, X)` SimpleITK grid via `Invertd(..., nearest_interp=False)`.
   - **3D U-Net Continuous Probability Inversion**: `train_3dunet_200ep.py` trained and validated on `(128, 128, 128)` trilinearly resized volumes without `Orientationd`. `evaluate_matched_200ep_models.py:299-300` thresholded probabilities at `0.5` *before* `Invertd(nearest_interp=True)`. Our adapter inverts continuous sigmoid probabilities `Invertd(..., nearest_interp=False)` to native resolution before applying the arm threshold, enabling fair threshold sweeping on the validation split in Arm C.
   - **V-Net Window Divisibility & Eval Mode**: `VNet` has 4 stride-2 downsampling stages requiring window sizes divisible by 16, and `RASNet.decode` returns `(logits, ds_outputs)` when `self.training` is `True`. `benchmark/adapters/monai_models.py` enforces `net.eval()` prior to inference and rounds any `VNet` `roi_size` up to the nearest multiple of 16 per axis.
   - **nnU-Net V2 Native Predictor**: Verified `checkpoint_best.pth` (`SHA-256: 9accddf283b1c480e386f30f426b9200ca8d4aa1ecd70d5b5af0b8aad6058711`) contains `init_args.plans` (`SHA-256: c67af4aaeb24f5a72b58c83e351a9c62cf026f8baf1bbb727e3022491d5733d8`) and `init_args.dataset_json` (`numTraining: 784`, `SHA-256: d81f40253d7c2d88b9d1cdbd4bd7319a7a0206fce5b2350287317bd34f9c7389`), trainer `nnUNetTrainer_200epochs`, configuration `3d_fullres` (target spacing `[0.5, 0.359375, 0.359375]`, patch size `[128, 192, 192]`, `CTNormalization`, `inference_allowed_mirroring_axes=(0, 1, 2)`). `benchmark/adapters/nnunet_adapter.py` uses native `nnUNetPredictor` directly on SimpleITK `(Z, Y, X)` arrays and reversed spacing `(sz, sy, sx)`.
   - **Unified MONAI Single/TTA Pass Execution (`infer_monai_both_passes`)**: `benchmark/adapters/monai_models.py` defines `infer_monai_both_passes` as the single authoritative implementation for both single-pass and 4-pass spatial flip TTA (`axes=(2, 3, 4)`), running under `torch.amp.autocast("cuda")` when a CUDA device is active, verifying `np.isfinite` on all inverted probability arrays, and returning `(prob_single_zyx, prob_tta_zyx)`. Both `infer_probability` and `benchmark/run_benchmark.py` call `infer_monai_both_passes`.
   - **Unified `nnU-Net V2` Batch Iterator & Parity Verification**: `benchmark/run_benchmark.py` executes `_run_nnunet_batch_probabilities` via `predictor.data_iterator_from_raw_npy_data` for all case counts (including 3-case smoke runs), asserting `int(ofile) == int(cid)` and `np.isfinite(prob)` for every volume. On the 3 validation smoke cases (`14, 45, 47`), the batch iterator (`smoke_verified_v2`) matches single-case `infer_nnunet_probability` (`smoke_val_all5`) within `7.75e-06` on Dice (`< 1e-4` tolerance).
   - **Surface Extraction Connectivity Convention (`surface-v1`)**: `benchmark/metrics.py` extracts 3D boundary surfaces via `mask & ~binary_erosion(mask, structure=generate_binary_structure(3, 1), border_value=0)` (6-connected face-adjacent erosion with `border_value=0` so image-border foreground voxels remain surface voxels) and computes exact physical surface-to-surface distances via `distance_transform_edt(~surface, sampling=spacing_zyx)`, verified against brute-force `scipy.spatial.distance.cdist` in `tests/test_benchmark_metrics.py`.
   - **Validation Threshold Grid Boundary Observation (`results/thresholds.json`)**: On the 94-case validation sweep over $\tau \in [0.30, 0.70]$ (step `0.05`) with top-2 connected-component pruning, `nnunet` (`0.8075` at $\tau^*=0.35$) and `segresnet` (`0.7901` at $\tau^*=0.35$) achieved interior maxima, whereas `rasnet` (`0.7897` at $\tau^*=0.30$), `vnet` (`0.7702` at $\tau^*=0.30$), and `3dunet` (`0.6047` at $\tau^*=0.30$) selected the lower boundary of the pre-registered grid (`0.30`). Because `StenosisAwareLoss` ($\gamma=2.5$) produces conservative foreground probabilities, thresholds below `0.30` were not explored in order to honor the frozen pre-registration.
