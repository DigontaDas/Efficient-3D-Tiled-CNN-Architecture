**Strengths:**

- **High Recall**: Mean recall of 0.784 suggests good sensitivity to vessel structures
- **Boundary Accuracy**: Best cases achieve HD95 = 1.0mm, demonstrating precise boundary localization
- **Robust to Scale**: Performance maintained across varying vessel sizes and contrast levels

**Weaknesses:**
